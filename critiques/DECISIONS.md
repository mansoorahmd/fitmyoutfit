# Founder decisions after the adversarial review

Decided 10 Nov 2026. These decisions answer the open questions in [SYNTHESIS.md](SYNTHESIS.md). plan.md has not been updated yet.

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Markets | US, UK, Pakistan and UAE, all four at launch |
| Production | A network of 20+ partner factories that already make these garments, not one owned factory |
| First segment | Teams and clubs |
| Company outside Pakistan | Our existing UAE company takes card and PayPal payments. Still to confirm: that it qualifies for Stripe and PayPal, and whether it can act as importer of record for UK and US duties |
| Single pieces | Pakistan only. Export orders have a minimum quantity |
| Factory per design | Not locked. Any free factory can take an order or reorder |
| Quality check | Personalized team orders go through our hub in Lahore. Plain bulk is checked at the factory, with photos |
| Remake guarantee | Written: a free replacement for our errors, in the customer's hands within 7 days |
| 3D editor | Build our own now |
| Who pays on team orders | The organizer pays. The team link collects names, numbers and sizes, not payments |
| Deposit | 50% at proof approval, 50% before shipping |
| Design help | Free for teams |
| Fit kits | Refundable deposit |
| Published turnaround | 15 days from proof approval to delivery, plus a paid rush tier |
| Export shipping | One box to the organizer, duty paid (DDP), each jersey bagged and labelled per player |
| Launch products | Jersey, T-shirt and jacket |
| Platform | Stay with Medusa. Keep the 3D editor and team-order logic loosely coupled, so a later move to Shopify costs less |
| Payments in Pakistan | JazzCash, Easypaisa and cards. Cash on delivery only for small orders, below a set limit |
