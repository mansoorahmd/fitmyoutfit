stand-in-tshirt.glb: licence notice
====================================

The garment geometry in stand-in-tshirt.glb comes from "Garment 3D" by Prashant Verma:

  Source:    https://github.com/prashant3285/garment (file shirt.glb, "GLB exported from CLO")
  Copyright: Copyright (c) 2021 Prashant Verma
  Licence:   MIT License (text below)

What we changed: scripts/process-stand-in-model.ts keeps only the outer cloth surface of each of
the seven pattern pieces (dropping the inner surface and the edge strips of CLO's thickness export),
removes all textures and the denim materials and puts one plain white material in their place,
renames the meshes after the pattern pieces, repacks the UVs into one 0 to 1 layout at one scale,
welds the seam vertices, recomputes the normals, adds baked ambient occlusion (_OCCLUSION) and
moves the garment to stand on Y = 0. The source file's SHA-256 is recorded in
stand-in-tshirt.source.json. It is a stand-in until our own pattern-based models arrive.
